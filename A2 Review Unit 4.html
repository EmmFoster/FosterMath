Question 8 · Graph & Solutions

x ≈ −0.5 and x ≈ −3.5
  • Vertex form: the vertex is (−2, −3); a = 2 > 0, so the graph opens up.
  • Choose x = −1: y = 2(1)² − 3 = −1 → (−1, −1); reflect: (−3, −1).
  • Choose x = 0: y = 2(2)² − 3 = 5 → (0, 5); reflect: (−4, 5).
  • The solutions of the equation are the x-intercepts.
  • The graph crosses the x-axis between 0 and −1 and between −3 and −4.
  • The solutions are approximately −0.5 and −3.5.
